Hello, I'm Huiyan Wang!

I’m a Chinese-born, London-based illustrator and animator who also enjoys
working with ceramics.

I’ve collaborated with a wide range of clients — from small non-profits to
global brands — to bring their ideas to life through illustration and animation.

I transform messages into visual stories, using playful elements, vibrant colour
palettes, and dynamic motion. I also explore interactive animation to add depth
and liveliness to my work.

My work has been recognized by several international awards, including:
– World Illustration Awards (Shortlist)
– Hiii Illustration Awards (Merit Award)
– iJungle Illustration Awards (Merit Award)
– Creative Quarterly 52 (Illustration Professional Runner-up)
– AI-AP International Motion Art Award (Student Winner)

For collaborations, project inquiries, or just to say hello, feel free to get in touch
via email — I’d love to hear from you!
